# Local CLI approval opening and expiry

Status: accepted by the project owner on 2026-11-22 at `adb7d9bce5aded0dedba3d9e8e98afec2d5919b4` under [#1737](https://github.com/realkarych/kora/issues/1737). [Owner acceptance](https://github.com/realkarych/kora/issues/1737#issuecomment-7081993174)

This contract covers opening and expiring the local CLI approval of the [walking skeleton](first-slice.md#skeleton-depth). The API is the CLI channel and reads the existing request and wait. Public reads and human responses, API/CLI/MCP parity, external channel effects, and VCS-bound approval keep their existing delivery owners. The later first slice uses VCS-bound CLI approvals. The skeleton installation checks the channel and the absence of authored `binds` independently at deployment and before opening. Sections 1 and 3 define the exact installation refusals and runtime outcomes. A mismatch with a saved deployment refuses a fresh transition before effects. Checking `channel = cli` alone does not establish an unbound approval.

The automatic-response audit rule is a separate [ADR-0006 amendment](../../docs/decisions/0006-graph-definition-dsl-and-yaml-representation.md#automatic-cli-approval-expiry-audit).

## 1. Source and opening

At deployment require exactly one input declaration on the top-level `human_input`. It has type `CommandResult`, names the top-level command as `from` and its `result` output as `output`, omits `path`, and omits `optional` or sets it to false. Omitted `config.context` selects that sole input. An explicit context is supported only if the checked path is directly the same `inputs` member, by member or equivalent literal-string selection of its exact input name, with no additional selector. Keep the exact authored context. No implicit ReviewBundle, projection, or rewrite of a supplied selector occurs. The shared validation used by validate and deploy applies the same installation check and returns `IR028` at the unsupported input, context, channel or binds path. Malformed paths retain their ordinary invalid-IR diagnostics. The [walking-skeleton manual start](first-slice.md#skeleton-depth) repeats that installation check against the complete selected pinned version with fresh installed facts before committing any new run, receipt, input or event. A proved unsupported binding is `API003` with the same diagnostic. Failure to obtain a complete authoritative selected version or check is the existing unavailable refusal. The current authentication and durable receipt comparison keep their precedence. An authorized exact accepted or skipped receipt replays before a fresh compatibility check, and a matching key with different content keeps `API004`. Authorized legacy history keeps its saved interpretation. Neither read nor replay activates an incompatible version for a fresh start. Fresh opening repeats the binding checks on the exact saved source and refuses a different, absent, transformed or ambiguous source before effects.

The predecessor must be succeeded after physical settlement. Select its original current attempt and exact current accepted artifact version. The accepted artifact and its validation, original attempt/grant/runtime provenance and exact saved contract must agree. The root payload, both accepted captures, identity summary and matching frozen CEL derived owner must be available under their own retention and authority rules. A ready capture, ordinary runner observation, recorded terminal report, digest or available object does not prove this complete source.

The [result acceptance producer](execution-content.md#52-command-result) owns the root and adopts both captures in one transaction. It binds the original runtime's trusted observation and sealed captures, through the authenticated Worker relay and complete current-authority/address checks. The producer supplies typed content and projection owners with bounded native reads. Scheduler settlement #1472 verifies complete closure, output authority and the durable original report, then owns successful completion. Opening reads those facts and does not accept a result, recapture logs, resolve storage references from candidate text or interpret runtime closure itself.

Prepare the bounded current source, exact saved effective form and checked predicate, and required original/converted CEL inputs outside the writer. Read the form and predicate from the complete pinned execution binding, with an explicit saved v2 output policy for this new transition. Legacy graph bindings retain their authorized history. Readers do not supply a current default or reinterpret v1 as v2. A proved missing or unsupported pinned policy/schema for this installation ends the eligible node with `config_error` and reason `approval_contract_unsupported` under the original writer, without a request or wait. A permanently exhausted bound on those fixed saved inputs is distinct and uses `approval_contract_exhausted`. These proposed outcomes require a complete fresh binding decision and follow the compact failure ownership below. A transient source or admission refusal follows the durable retry rule below. A payload, preview and typed CEL projection are distinct sources. Read only scoped owner identities and availability for captures; opening never loads either capture body. Use the accepted owner's native read admission before loading any needed context or projection content. Release the read snapshot before payload parsing, CEL preparation or storage work. Approval acquisition needs its own migration and maintenance qualification on PostgreSQL; earlier skeleton read exceptions apply only to their named acquisitions. A fresh writer verifies the same run, pin, current node/predecessor, accepted owner, context identities and availability under the original run lock and the address phase in section 4. Prepared content confers no transition authority by itself.

A ready candidate may still be stored as `pending` when the scheduler's readiness check is part of opening. Before any write, verify its exact declared readiness, no unresolved earlier current wait for the node, and original parent deadline eligibility. Commit one open `wait` of kind `approval` with `attempt_id` NULL, one pending `approval_request`, a waiting node, its exact consumed context version, one versioned `approval.requested` event, and `run.needs_scheduling = true` in the same transaction. The [event catalogue](data-model.md#local-cli-approval-event-variants) defines its scheduler provenance and complete payload. The request stores its original `wait_id`, the versioned effective `form` below, `context_artifact_version_id`, `run_cost_version = 1` and full saved `run_cost`. The graph pin, node ID and checked saved predicate identify its durable expression; the transition does not copy expression text into delivery metadata. `bound_commit_sha`, `bound_digests`, `observed_head_sha`, `channel_request_id`, `response`, `approver` and `evaluation_error` are NULL on creation. The wait's ordinary unused observation, subscription, reason and evaluation-error fields are also NULL. There is no attempt, execution occupancy or coroutine per wait.

Sample the opening time from the injected clock after lock admission. `wake_at` is that time plus the exact checked saved timeout, in the existing UTC timestamp precision and duration semantics. The existing enclosing deadline and latch take precedence. If the authoritative time is available and the checked authored duration yields an unrepresentable `wake_at`, the proposed permanent disposition fails the eligible node with `config_error` and reason `approval_timeout_unrepresentable`, marks the run, and creates no wait, request, hold, attempt or retry. It does not replace or move the enclosing deadline. A temporarily unavailable clock or inability to observe the enclosing authority is a refusal, preserves the candidate's scheduling mark, and does not record that configuration failure. The original bound is immutable. Candidate discovery, delivery, replay, and restart never recalculate it. A fresh exact replay while the original pair owns the node returns that pair without changing its deadline, context, form, cost, hold or event. A mismatched pair, a superseded source or a lost admissible dependency cannot attach to the old request. A terminal pair never reopens. A later eligible node lifecycle requires a new exact request/wait identity under its governing retry rules.

Call [the existing RunCost calculator](data-model.md#read-only-approval-aggregation-1517) once in the original locked writer. Persist its exact result with the request. Unpriced, partial and missing-report states in a valid snapshot are data. An unusable source or incomplete acquisition leaves the transition uncommitted with an unavailable classification. A proved overflow instead fails the scheduler-only node atomically with `config_error` and reason `approval_cost_too_large`, marks the run, and creates no request, wait, hold or attempt. The compact durable failure owner for this scope is the corresponding scheduler-authored `node.failed` event linked to the exact run and node by its indexed identity. For these opening failures use the [versioned CLI node failure event](data-model.md#local-cli-approval-event-variants) with `config_error` and reason `approval_cost_too_large`, `approval_contract_unsupported`, `approval_contract_exhausted` or `approval_timeout_unrepresentable`. There is no cost payload on the event and no change to the ledger. The future selected node-failure read owns resolving and pinning that event. The caller exits the original transaction on every path so native portals actually close.

Temporary refusal before opening leaves the eligible node without a pair and retains `run.needs_scheduling = true`. The final check in #1448 treats an unresolved recoverable preparation or admission refusal as remaining work, even if there is no wait yet. It uses a fresh locked eligibility check and cannot acknowledge the marker based on that refusal. It retries on later finite scheduling rounds; it does not loop until a provider becomes available. A restart uses the durable marker and real readiness facts to rediscover that same candidate.

The installation's scheduler owns one shared bounded preparation admission for opening and automatic continuation. A candidate earns no queue entry. Reserve a slot and the conservative aggregate operation allowance before checkout, native field acquisition or detached work. Account for simultaneous backend/driver and detached copies of the saved graph, form, context and CEL projections, the bounded default/response copy, v2 validation and projection work, and private result buffers. Do not admit each phase against the same credit while its predecessor still retains a copy. Use existing per-value and per-expression bounds as inputs to the aggregate reservation package. The runtime plan must name this finite installation configuration and qualify it on both databases before activation; this specification selects no new count or process-wide measurement gate.

Saturation refuses before acquiring metadata or content or submitting work. The candidate remains durable by its mark before opening or its latched continuation after expiry. Exit the read snapshot before detached work while still holding the same operation reservation. Transfer only bounded prepared facts to the guarded writer. Complete or discard them and release admission only after the writer, noninterruptible evaluation, native reads, provider work and all owned copies actually settle. Cancellation, timeout or scheduler loop restart cannot reissue still-held capacity. Restart of the loop uses the same installation owner and verifies actual shutdown/closure under #1448. Unsettled work and its charge remain with that owner until affirmative release. Future public response processing needs its own finite allocation and concurrent installation accounting in its implementing plan; it does not silently borrow this scheduler reservation.

## 2. CLI delivery record and discovery

The v1 `approval_request.delivery` is a closed JSON document. Its members are as follows.

| Member | Required value |
|---|---|
| `version` | Integer `1`. |
| `marker` | The original approval request ID. |
| `resource` | Closed object with `kind = local_cli`, `namespace_id` equal to the original run namespace ID, and integer `binding_revision = 1`. The binding is the built-in channel in that metadata namespace. |
| `desired_state` | `open` for a live request or `closed` for a closed request. |
| `revision` | Integer `1` with `open`, integer `2` with `closed`, including an expired request whose automatic default is still due. |

The marker follows the row's exact ID and the resource follows the original run namespace, using existing identifier domains. The binding revision identifies this fixed local channel in this format. It does not follow an external integration alias or current token. The compact record is at most 1,024 canonical ASCII bytes; admit its native field before loading JSON and refuse inconsistent or extra members. A local request has no external dispatch authorization, learned ID, polling token, provider call, or reconciliation cursor. Keep `channel_request_id` NULL. The request/wait/delivery rows and current namespace authority govern the local API read. A closed, historical or expired request offers no new response right. Historical reads preserve older row meanings. SQL NULL, unknown versions and legacy unversioned records cannot open a new delivery or supply a new response authority; do not backfill an open record from status alone.

The `delivery.version` discriminates the local CLI fields from historical form and response meanings. For a v1 row, `approval_request.form` is a closed object with integer `version = 1` and `canonical_base64`. The latter contains canonical padded standard Base64 of the saved effective form's canonical UTF-8 JSON, using the existing selected schema-source bound of 1,048,576 bytes and a maximum of 1,398,104 Base64 characters. The reader validates canonical Base64 by exact decoded length and re-encoding, checks this envelope against the original checked binding, and projects the logical form to authorized callers. The representation carries the exact snapshot on both databases, including strings which cannot be stored directly in PostgreSQL JSONB. Do not decode unversioned historical forms as v1.

Read the current pair through bounded exact-owner lookups. Index open approval waits by `(node_instance_id, id)` and pending CLI requests by `(wait_id, id)`, with status, kind or channel as appropriate in each predicate. Retrieve at most two matching IDs at each lookup so duplicates refuse without loading both records' content. Require an exact cross-match to the same node, run, namespace, wait and request, and fresh original time and binding rechecks in the writer. Discovery and authority checks use scalar identities. Form, response, cost and context fields have independent native admission before acquisition and never join a due page. Each form or response envelope has at most 1,398,360 canonical ASCII bytes, including its metadata. Use the saved RunCost bound and each actual content owner's bound for the other fields. An oversized or unsupported persisted field refuses before decoding. Charge simultaneous native and decoding copies to the admitted preparation owner in section 1. The original run lock serializes pair creation and all closing paths; discovery never chooses a winner among duplicate rows.

Discover ordinary expiry through a separate keyset over the partial index on `(id, wake_at)` for open approval waits with non-NULL `wake_at`. Close the owning snapshot at every page. At the start of a sweep, capture a UTC cutoff and the largest indexed ID in the same snapshot. Then read a bounded native page of scalar `id` and `wake_at`, with `last_id < id <= ceiling` and `ORDER BY id`. The first page has no lower cursor. The complete partial-index predicate and the leading ID range determine membership in that page. The native select takes at most the established 100 rows, before due filtering or any owner join. Qualify the actual leading-range access and count all raw entries selected before filtering.

After admitting the raw page, keep ordinary due candidates whose `wake_at` is at or before the frozen cutoff. Advance to the last native ID even when every row is future-due or every subsequent candidate refuses. An empty due list is not end of sweep; raw exhaustion ends the sweep. Translate each retained candidate identity through a bounded owner lookup after the page transaction closes. Use a fresh original writer check of exact owner, status, original `wake_at`, `now >= wake_at`, and enclosing latches. The two timestamp tests select and then authorize work. Neither moves the original deadline or orders completion by deadline.

The ID-first range excludes later greater IDs using the leading key. A writer that inserts behind the cursor, or a row that becomes due after the captured cutoff, is visited in a later sweep. Normal monotone arrivals do not extend the fixed ceiling. A new issuer after restart may allocate IDs within the previous range; advancement uses exact raw IDs without assuming global allocation order. Restart discards the cursor; as long as the scheduler continues processing finite pages, already present rows in the admitted interval progress past future waits and refused candidates. A clock rollback prevents early unlatched expiry in the fresh writer. An authoritative sticky observation continues through the separate source below. The tradeoff is native work over open approval waits including future ones. Unrelated terminal history is outside the partial index and there is no claim of deadline-priority ordering or a wall-clock bound on the sweep.

Discover latched automatic continuations independently of wall-clock eligibility. Use a partial index on wait ID for the exact v1 kind/tag over open approval waits, with its own indexed ID ceiling and leading-range keyset pages of at most 100. Advance on raw ID, including refused rows, and validate the complete saved latch in the original writer. Exact pair and status prevent duplicate apply when both sources find the same owner. Discover rows left open by interrupted terminal-run cancellation through these original predicates. Commit graph effects with the scheduling marker. Independent discovery supplies candidates without a wake claim, update-time watermark or marker and grants no authority to acknowledge that marker. #1448 composes both sources into the complete restart and fixed-point algorithm.

## 3. Expiry and retained response

The original run lock serializes a current human response and automatic expiry. Use fresh time and the original parent latch under that lock. At equality with the enclosing deadline, apply the enclosing timeout first and cancel its approval wait without applying the default. Preserve earlier cancellation and terminal decisions. At equality with `wake_at`, an ordinary response is stale. Once ordinary expiry is durably observed, clock regression cannot restore delivery or response authority.

The durable observation is the exact `wait.reason` document `{version: 1, kind: "approval_expiry_observed", observed_at: <UTC>}`, using the existing canonical UTC text with six fractional digits and a terminal `Z`. Opening leaves this field SQL NULL. The observation writer uses a short metadata transaction under the original run lock, independent of hold settlement. After checking the exact current pair, the parent deadline and fresh `now >= wake_at`, it writes the immutable first observation, sets the request `expired`, closes delivery, revokes the current observation and marks the run. It does not acquire or clear the original native or physical address holds. Address/native closure refusal cannot delay or roll back a separately committed authority fence. The original held data, charges and authority to close the original holds remain with their exact owners through actual settlement in section 4. It also saves one [versioned `approval.expired` event](data-model.md#local-cli-approval-event-variants), under the scheduler system actor at the observed time. That event describes expiry, before any delayed node resolution. A scan, a stale candidate or an uncommitted or rolled-back observation transaction is no durable observation and supplies no event. On uncertain metadata settlement, recover the original outcome before claiming the fence or retrying the write. The original transaction either committed both the fence and the event or left neither. Legacy or malformed reason values confer no latch authority. Any entry point that discovers a current due pair under the authoritative lock uses the same writer, even when later preparation will refuse. The future human route applies its existing authorization and `API006`/unapplied audit behavior for such a stale response.

The recovery rule for an uncertain automatic default expiry observation requires actual settlement of the original transaction. Where qualified custody applies, the original Database writer or reserved recovery owner establishes the exact original outcome and affirmative native closure under the [custody contract](execution-custody-native.md#34-native-release-outcome-and-recovery). Later work must pass the [existing qualified admission checks](execution-custody-recovery-priority.md#one-ordered-recovery-request). Visible approval rows confer no native outcome or closure proof. Unresolved original proof prevents automatic result admission, a repeated expiry observation and release of the original holds.

After settlement, use bounded reads keyed by the original request and wait IDs under the original run lock. The exact current pair establishes the committed domain observation when the wait has the exact valid persisted latch and the original request is `expired` with matching closed delivery. Recovery checks that committed state without reading audit history. An absent or inconsistent witness grants no authority to admit a result or repeat the observation. After a proved rollback, a new observation must pass the ordinary fresh due and authority checks.

The observation completes `fail` and `skip` in the same transaction. For `default_value`, settle the independent observation transaction first, including the fence and the single original expiry event. Only after its commit is verified may automatic default processing acquire native result resources or apply its response and output in a later writer. Already prepared values may remain with their original owners through actual settlement. Their availability supplies no authority to admit the result or join its acceptance to the observation transaction. A later refusal or rollback leaves the committed fence and expiry event intact. The request stays `expired` and delivery closed, while the wait stays open as a scheduler continuation for the exact latched expiry. It is absent from pending human-request discovery and sets no authorization. Its index and the original scheduling marker keep it actionable after clock regression or restart. Preparation starts from current bounded reads and acquires independent read ownership. Outstanding holds still protect their original data until release. An expired request and its retained holds confer no delivery or response access. The final locked writer validates the same latch, original source, exact pair and enclosing eligibility. An enclosing cancellation or timeout can still close the unfinished wait; it leaves the expired audit and any original evidence intact. When expiry resolves the node, mark the wait `expired` without changing its first observation or emitting another expiry event.

| Saved policy or disposition | Node result |
|---|---|
| `fail` | Fail with `approval_expired`. The versioned node failure records exactly `error_class = approval_expired` and `reason = approval_expired`. |
| `skip` | Skip the node. No response output is created. |
| True predicate for a complete valid `default_value` | Succeed with an accepted response output. |
| False predicate for a complete valid `default_value` | Fail with `approval_rejected` and preserve the validated response. The versioned node failure records exactly `error_class = approval_rejected` and `reason = approval_rejected`. |
| CEL error evaluating a complete valid `default_value` | Fail with `config_error` and `cel_evaluation_error`, with the safe error record and retained response. |
| Missing authored default | Fail with `config_error` and `approval_default_missing`. Explicit JSON null is an actual value subject to validation. |
| Completed invalid form/value decision | Fail with `config_error` and `approval_default_invalid`. No response output. |
| Definitive saved-policy/schema refusal or deterministic exhaustion of a bound for the same fixed saved value | Fail with `config_error` and the distinct `approval_default_unsupported` or `approval_default_exhausted` reason. Neither means that the evaluated value failed its schema. No response output. |
| Transient source, resource admission or writer refusal | Preserve the original latched continuation and mark. No default result, new rights or moved deadline. Retry through bounded scheduling. |

Classify a complete validation result as invalid only when it actually evaluated and failed. Proved unsupported policy/schema and deterministic policy work/shape exhaustion remain refusals. Temporary inability to acquire a slot, read or lock does not establish either permanent disposition. An unavailable frozen input to a completed predicate evaluation uses the existing `projection_unavailable` error. The automatic error is recorded with the node's failure facts using the exact safe [IR error record](ir.md#77-evaluation-errors). For an automatic CEL error, the [versioned CLI node failure event](data-model.md#local-cli-approval-event-variants) binds the exact run and node and records `config_error`, reason `cel_evaluation_error` and that exact safe error record in `details`. `approval_request.evaluation_error` stays NULL for automatic expiry. It remains the ordinary failed-human-response field; preserve that event, selected-field and legacy meaning. Apply the [ADR-0006 audit exception](../../docs/decisions/0006-graph-definition-dsl-and-yaml-representation.md#automatic-cli-approval-expiry-audit) only for v1 local default expiry.

Use one response preparation and result-application owner. Direct and any future polled ingress apply their existing authentication, authorization and strict approval input domain, including required checks on unused integer fields. Polled apply additionally requires the current observation authority. Stale or mismatched human input never evaluates the predicate. Automatic ingress takes the exact saved default through the same strict value checks, effective schema, checker and projection routines. It requires the current due pair and never acquires human-response authority. Reuse the saved [standard output policy](schema-validation.md), checked effective form, [CEL projection path sets](data-model.md#saved-cel-projection-path-sets) and [detached StructuredOutput producer](data-model.md#lossless-artifact-cel-projection-storage). In addition to the frozen response-output cut, prepare and bound the response reads needed by the checked predicate with its other pinned inputs. Evaluate the predicate only after fresh writer authority checks over the admitted compact inputs. Automatic actions use the existing scheduler transaction allowance. API evaluations keep the existing [per-expression budget outside that allowance](ir.md#76-data-bounds-cost-and-evaluation). Neither logical bound guarantees wall-clock completion or interruption of an executing expression. Whole-document validation and storage work stay outside the writer.

The final original writer rechecks authority and samples the clock after predicate evaluation, any native or address admission waits, and any other wait that can affect acceptance, immediately before the response, current-output and outcome writes. It rechecks the original current owner, enclosing deadline and sticky latch. A human response must also still hold a current pending request and open wait with `now < wake_at` and no expiry observation, plus the still-valid current approver and source bindings under the existing authorization contract and applicable observation token. Equality fails the applicable check. If a later step must wait again before acceptance, it repeats the final guard before applying the result. An enclosing timeout wins for either origin and uses the existing ordered timeout and cancellation transition. A human cutoff crossed during processing uses the ordinary stale-response path and authoritative expiry observation. Discard the newly evaluated response/output on either refusal, without publishing the value, writing an applied response audit or succeeding the node. An already latched automatic action keeps its original due semantics after clock rollback and still needs fresh enclosing eligibility at acceptance.

A successful approval response is a scheduler-produced StructuredOutput with `attempt_id` SQL NULL. It has no Worker grant, physical request, capture or Worker upload staging. The [StructuredOutput activation owners](artifacts/StructuredOutput.md#5-verification-and-activation) (work items 8, 10 and 12, coordinated through #138) must supply a real nonphysical content acceptance path before #1447 activates. Its handoff accepts an admitted canonical response and checked projection with the original run, node, output contract, wait, approval request, and terminal-response origin. The final writer can commit the exact scheduler-owned artifact, accepted content and native projection owners, lineage, and result once with the current wait transition. It needs bounded native acquisition, independent payload/projection availability, existing retention rules and actual cancellation/release evidence. The exact physical representation, owner and compatibility plan need a separate approval. Existing physical Worker staging or an audit response cannot stand in for this material dependency. This producer has no dependency on the public approval surface, a previously shipped #1447 opening or the #1448 loop. Develop it against the shared engine boundary and qualify the first relying acceptance together with the actual transition. Do not activate a waiting request whose configured default path lacks the producer.

The v1 response in `approval_request.response` is SQL NULL until a complete valid response is applied. The closed JSON envelope contains integer `version = 1`, `origin = submitted` or `expiry_default`, and `canonical_base64`. The latter is canonical padded standard Base64 of the exact canonical UTF-8 StructuredOutput bytes of the admitted typed response snapshot, at most 1,048,576 decoded bytes and 1,398,104 characters. Freeze the snapshot after existing ingress normalization; the envelope preserves that snapshot across SQLite and PostgreSQL, including JSON null. The bounded native reader checks its exact length, version, member set and Base64 re-encoding. The existing authorized `response` selector projects only the logical response and its existing availability semantics, without adding an origin field. It refuses corrupt or unsupported content. Public activation stays with the approval surface. Keep complete valid applied responses even when the predicate returns false or errors. The audit envelope confers no artifact acceptance or authority.

Automatic processing always keeps the request `expired` and `approver` NULL. It never writes `approval.responded`. The first `approval.expired` event stays an expiry observation; the later node event owns the default outcome and its error. A human response keeps its existing independent audit, including `failed` and request `evaluation_error` on an applied CEL error. Repeating observation or resuming an already resolved wait adds no event, hold change or response output.

## 4. Address ownership and closure

Only the exact pending current CLI request with an open wait and open delivery can authorize delivery of its form and bound context. The original protection and accounting can remain after authority closes. The form is the immutable inline saved value and needs no separate store address. Name the root, both command-capture owners, and content-bearing summary by their exact accepted owner identities. Include the existing projection owner's exact identity in the bound context and protect its native retained payload while held. Under the run lock and original ordered scopes, acquire the unique complete ordered set of physical addresses of those owners, plus any additional physical addresses written by the same transition, before locking/modifying saved owners. Derive the set from admitted storage identity and full digest, independent of aliases. Missing/unavailable sources, address-generation mismatch, or a pending deletion fence prevent the new hold. Recheck the exact context manifest under the lock. The [shared address eligibility](execution-content.md#24-eligibility-and-indexes) guards every owner writer and delete admission.

Extend the shared hold relation for this purpose. The key is the exact approval request ID, accepted content owner type and ID, and reason `approval_delivery`, with the complete physical address for physical content and an exact native derived owner for the CEL view. This new reason and owner predicate need explicit approval. Keep an address-leading live-hold lookup and a request-leading owner lookup. The same physical address may represent several accepted owners. Release each of this request's holds without removing the protection of another live holder or an independently eligible owner. Inline form delivery eligibility follows the same current request/wait/revision predicate. Releasing that eligibility removes the form from delivery, while retaining its authorized historical snapshot.

Opening acquires these holds with revision 1. The first authoritative ordinary expiry sets revision 2 and revokes any current observation, delivery or response authority in its own committed writer. Original held protection, charges and close authority persist until a later transaction settles those exact holds. Direct response, supersession, cancellation and enclosing expiry retain the existing ordered close path where no ordinary expiry is already latched. If those paths encounter an existing expiry fence, they preserve the fence, audit and outstanding release ownership.

The release writer joins the closed original request's live holds by their retained identities under the original run lock and rechecks the exact closed intent, every held identity, address and native owner before writes. Candidate discovery comes from the live `approval_delivery` hold index keyed by `(request_id, owner_type, owner_id)`, including closed waits and terminal runs. The full tuple is both the leading native cursor and the frozen upper key for a sweep. Use the same finite raw-page discipline as section 2, then check the closed intent. Later sweeps rediscover refused holders regardless of the wait state, clock or scheduling mark. Release advances no approval revision and appends no approval event. On native/address refusal or writer rollback, retain the entire original charged hold union and the exact close authority. Resume the same release after restart without restoring delivery, rewriting the original expiry, or relying on a still-open wait or active run. A normal request owns at most four logical physical content holds (root, stdout, stderr and summary) and one native projection hold, independent of aliases or the size of retained history. It refuses a partial union. Cancellation stays ordered by the enclosing scope's original sticky latch; incomplete closure remains due until finished. Replaying an already settled release changes no revision, audit, holds or charges. Recovery of an unsettled release stays with its original owner. If an already prepared user response or default loses authority, dispose of its private preparation once actual work/reads close. The existing [#1444 cancellation writer](scheduler.md#metadata-expiry-and-recovery-implementation-1444) must be composed with this typed close for v1; legacy simple JSON updates cannot perform this release. Release changes hold eligibility and cannot delete bytes, renew retention, revoke other owners, or clear publication or physical execution reservations. An expired default continuation holds no delivery authority. Pending original hold settlement, independent reads and prepared work each retain their own custody and release obligations.

Every attempt to open or close has one finite admitted deadline for its metadata and detached preparation phases, present through actual native disposal. Whole-document validation, reads of artifact payloads and provider calls stay outside metadata resources. Use bounded native read acquisition and dispose before acquiring host or provider resources. No failure, timeout or restart is proof that an outstanding reader, writer, portal or effect released.
