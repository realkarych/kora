# Local CLI approval opening and expiry

Status: proposed under [#1737](https://github.com/realkarych/kora/issues/1737), 2026-11-21. The owner approved preparation and independent review. The record formats, automatic expiry audit rule, and runtime implementation still require owner approval. [Authorization](https://github.com/realkarych/kora/issues/1447#issuecomment-7063408372)

This contract covers opening and expiring the local CLI approval of the [walking skeleton](first-slice.md#skeleton-depth). The API is the CLI channel and reads the existing request and wait. Public reads and human responses, API/CLI/MCP parity, external channel effects, and VCS-bound approval keep their existing delivery owners. The later first slice uses VCS-bound CLI approvals. The skeleton installation checks the channel and the absence of authored `binds` independently at deployment and before opening. Report unsupported authored settings through the existing installation refusal. A mismatch with a saved deployment refuses a fresh transition before effects. Checking `channel = cli` alone does not establish an unbound approval.

[ADR-0003](../../docs/decisions/0003-scheduler-and-workers-over-http.md), [ADR-0006](../../docs/decisions/0006-graph-definition-dsl-and-yaml-representation.md), [ADR-0008](../../docs/decisions/0008-content-bound-approvals.md), [ADR-0018](../../docs/decisions/0018-immutable-attempt-history.md), [ADR-0021](../../docs/decisions/0021-external-observations-outside-transactions.md), [ADR-0022](../../docs/decisions/0022-inherited-execution-deadlines.md), and [ADR-0027](../../docs/decisions/0027-bounded-effective-schema-validation.md) govern the existing authority and retention rules. The proposed automatic-response audit rule is a separate [ADR-0006 amendment](../../docs/decisions/0006-graph-definition-dsl-and-yaml-representation.md#proposed-automatic-cli-approval-expiry-audit).

## 1. Source and opening

The scheduler consumes exactly the saved direct input of the top-level `human_input` node: the `result` output of the declared command predecessor in the same run, with the pinned CommandResult revision-1 binding. The predecessor must be succeeded after physical settlement. Select its original current attempt and exact current accepted artifact version. The accepted artifact and its validation, original attempt/grant/runtime provenance and exact saved contract must agree. The root payload, both accepted captures, identity summary and matching frozen CEL derived owner must be available under their own retention and authority rules. A ready capture, ordinary runner observation, recorded terminal report, digest or available object does not prove this complete source.

The [result acceptance producer](execution-content.md#52-command-result) owns the root and adopts both captures in one transaction. It binds the original runtime's trusted observation and sealed captures, through the authenticated Worker relay and complete current-authority/address checks. The producer supplies typed content and projection owners with bounded native reads. Scheduler settlement #1472 verifies complete closure, output authority and the durable original report, then owns successful completion. Opening reads those facts and does not accept a result, recapture logs, resolve storage references from candidate text or interpret runtime closure itself.

Prepare the bounded current source, exact saved effective form and checked predicate, and required original/converted CEL inputs outside the writer. Read the form and predicate from the complete pinned execution binding, with an explicit saved v2 output policy for this new transition. Legacy graph bindings retain their authorized history. Missing compatible saved behavior refuses opening; readers do not supply a current default or reinterpret v1 as v2. A payload, preview and typed CEL projection are distinct sources. Read only scoped owner identities and availability for captures; opening never loads either capture body. Use the accepted owner's native read admission before loading any needed context or projection content. Release the read snapshot before payload parsing, CEL preparation or storage work. Approval acquisition needs its own migration and maintenance qualification on PostgreSQL; earlier skeleton read exceptions apply only to their named acquisitions. A fresh writer verifies the same run, pin, current node/predecessor, accepted owner, context identities and availability under the original run lock and the address phase in section 4. Prepared content confers no transition authority by itself.

A ready candidate may still be stored as `pending` when the scheduler's readiness check is part of opening. Before any write, verify its exact declared readiness, no unresolved earlier current wait for the node, and original parent deadline eligibility. Commit one open `wait` of kind `approval` with `attempt_id` NULL, one pending `approval_request`, a waiting node, its exact consumed context version, one `approval.requested` event, and `run.needs_scheduling = true` in the same transaction. The event uses the scheduler system actor and exactly `approval_id`, `wait_id` and `context_artifact_version_id` as its payload. The request stores its original `wait_id`, the versioned effective `form` below, `context_artifact_version_id`, `run_cost_version = 1` and full saved `run_cost`. The graph pin, node ID and checked saved predicate identify its durable expression; the transition does not copy expression text into delivery metadata. `bound_commit_sha`, `bound_digests`, `observed_head_sha`, `channel_request_id`, `response`, `approver` and `evaluation_error` are NULL on creation. The wait's ordinary unused observation, subscription, reason and evaluation-error fields are also NULL. There is no attempt, execution occupancy or coroutine per wait.

Sample the opening time from the injected clock after lock admission. `wake_at` is that time plus the exact checked saved timeout, in the existing UTC timestamp precision and duration semantics. Refuse an unrepresentable calculation before writes. The original bound is immutable. Candidate discovery, delivery, replay, and restart never recalculate it. A fresh exact replay while the original pair owns the node returns that pair without changing its deadline, context, form, cost, hold or event. A mismatched pair, a superseded source or a lost admissible dependency cannot attach to the old request. A terminal pair never reopens. A later eligible node lifecycle requires a new exact request/wait identity under its governing retry rules.

Call [the existing RunCost calculator](data-model.md#read-only-approval-aggregation-1517) once in the original locked writer. Persist its exact result with the request. Unpriced, partial and missing-report states in a valid snapshot are data. An unusable source or incomplete acquisition leaves the transition uncommitted with an unavailable classification. A proved overflow instead fails the scheduler-only node atomically with `config_error` and reason `approval_cost_too_large`, marks the run, and creates no request, wait, hold or attempt. The compact durable failure owner for this scope is the corresponding scheduler-authored `node.failed` event linked to the exact run and node by its indexed identity. Its payload contains exactly `node_path`, `error_class = config_error`, and `reason = approval_cost_too_large`. There is no cost payload on the event and no change to the ledger. The future selected node-failure read owns resolving and pinning that event. The caller exits the original transaction on every path so native portals actually close.

## 2. CLI delivery record and discovery

The proposed v1 `approval_request.delivery` is a closed JSON document. Its members are as follows.

| Member | Required value |
|---|---|
| `version` | Integer `1`. |
| `marker` | The original approval request ID. |
| `resource` | Closed object with `kind = local_cli`, `namespace_id` equal to the original run namespace ID, and integer `binding_revision = 1`. The binding is the built-in channel in that metadata namespace. |
| `desired_state` | `open` for a live request or `closed` for a closed request. |
| `revision` | Integer `1` with `open`, integer `2` with `closed`. |

The marker follows the row's exact ID and the resource follows the original run namespace, using existing identifier domains. The binding revision identifies this fixed local channel in this format. It does not follow an external integration alias or current token. The compact record is at most 1,024 canonical ASCII bytes; admit its native field before loading JSON and refuse inconsistent or extra members. A local request has no external dispatch authorization, learned ID, polling token, provider call, or reconciliation cursor. Keep `channel_request_id` NULL. The request/wait/delivery rows and current namespace authority govern the local API read. A closed, historical or expired request offers no new response right. Historical reads preserve older row meanings. SQL NULL, unknown versions and legacy unversioned records cannot open a new delivery or supply a new response authority; do not backfill an open record from status alone.

The `delivery.version` discriminates the local CLI fields from historical form and response meanings. For a v1 row, `approval_request.form` is a closed object with integer `version = 1` and `canonical_base64`. The latter contains canonical padded standard Base64 of the saved effective form's canonical UTF-8 JSON, using the existing selected schema-source bound of 1,048,576 bytes and a maximum of 1,398,104 Base64 characters. The reader validates canonical Base64 by exact decoded length and re-encoding, checks this envelope against the original checked binding, and projects the logical form to authorized callers. The representation carries the exact snapshot on both databases, including strings which cannot be stored directly in PostgreSQL JSONB. Do not decode unversioned historical forms as v1.

Read the current pair through bounded exact-owner lookups. Index open approval waits by `(node_instance_id, id)` and pending CLI requests by `(wait_id, id)`, with status, kind or channel as appropriate in each predicate. Retrieve at most two matching IDs at each lookup so duplicates refuse without loading both records' content. Require an exact cross-match to the same node, run, namespace, wait and request, and fresh original time and binding rechecks in the writer. Discovery and authority checks use scalar identities. Form, response, cost and context fields have independent native admission before acquisition and never join a due page. Each form or response envelope has at most 1,398,360 canonical ASCII bytes, including its metadata. Use the saved RunCost bound and each actual content owner's bound for the other fields. An oversized or unsupported persisted field refuses before decoding. Admit simultaneous native and decoding copies to a finite operation allowance; sample query work against growing unrelated history. The original run lock serializes pair creation and all closing paths; discovery never chooses a winner among duplicate rows.

Discover due work through an independent finite cursor over `(wake_at, id)` for open approval waits with a non-NULL `wake_at`. Its index is keyed on those columns for that predicate. Each sweep freezes its UTC cutoff and upper wait-ID ceiling, and each page returns at most 100 due identities in ascending tuple order. Release the discovery transaction before processing a page. Use a fresh writer check of current identity, status, original `wake_at`, `now >= wake_at`, and enclosing latches. Advance past candidates that refuse and process their still-due rows in a later sweep. Restart discards the cursor and uses the same durable predicate, including rows left open when terminal-run cancellation was interrupted. Mark ordinary scheduling for committed graph effects. The sweep does not depend on a wake claim, update-time watermark, or `needs_scheduling`, and grants no authority to acknowledge that marker. #1448 composes this source into the complete restart and fixed-point algorithm.

## 3. Expiry and retained response

The original run lock serializes a current human response and automatic expiry. Use fresh time and the original parent latch under that lock. At equality with the enclosing deadline, apply the enclosing timeout first, cancel its approval wait and close delivery without applying the default. Preserve earlier cancellation and terminal decisions. At equality with `wake_at` an ordinary response is stale even if expiry has yet to be processed. A refused human response follows the existing authority checks and `API006`/unapplied audit rules. Local polling supplies the same canonical request/wait information; any future polled apply additionally requires its already specified current observation authority.

On ordinary expiry, set the request to `expired`, close its delivery and release its delivery holds atomically with the new wait/node outcomes, audit and scheduling marker. Mark the wait `expired` in all three ordinary expiry paths. Apply the checked, saved policy as follows.

| Saved policy | Node result |
|---|---|
| `fail` | Fail with `approval_expired`. |
| `skip` | Skip the node. No response output is created. |
| `default_value` | Use the saved value as a scheduler-originated response under the same effective form and checked predicate. A true predicate succeeds with an accepted response output. A false predicate fails the node with `approval_rejected` and preserves the validated response. Evaluation failure preserves the validated response and fails the node with `config_error` and `cel_evaluation_error`. |

A missing authored default is a pinned configuration failure with `config_error` and reason `approval_default_missing`. An explicit JSON null is an actual value, subject to the same form and strict ingress checks. A complete invalid default fails with `config_error` and `approval_default_invalid`. Neither case creates a response output. A temporary inability to acquire an admitted source, validation slot or writer refuses the transition atomically. The original pair remains due for a later attempt, with the original hold owned until the closing transaction settles. Expiry time fences all new delivery or response authority during this interval. Such a refusal cannot complete the default or extend its deadline. An unavailable frozen input to a completed CEL evaluation is the specified `projection_unavailable` error. Preserve the content-free [IR error record](ir.md#77-evaluation-errors), including its exact field, offset and timing, and save it in `evaluation_error` and the failed node's facts under the same transition.

Use one response preparation and result-application owner. Direct and polled ingress apply their existing authentication/authorization and strict approval input domain, including required checks on unused integer fields. Stale/mismatched human input does not evaluate the predicate. Automatic ingress takes the exact saved default through the same strict value checks, effective schema, checker and projection routines. It requires the current due wait and never acquires human-response authority. Reuse the saved [standard output policy](schema-validation.md), the effective form chosen at deployment, the existing [CEL projection path sets](data-model.md#saved-cel-projection-path-sets), and the [detached StructuredOutput producer](data-model.md#lossless-artifact-cel-projection-storage). In addition to the frozen response-output cut, prepare and bound the local response reads needed by the checked predicate with its other pinned inputs. Apply the checked predicate under the original writer after fresh authority checks and within the existing scheduler CEL allowance, over the admitted compact inputs. No storage or provider call or whole-document validation spans this writer.

Where a response output needs provider-backed preparation, the shared output owner first admits its private staging under the same original request/wait authority and current source bounds, after confirming precise response or expiry eligibility. Qualified custody owns storage work and confirmed cleanup outside the writer. The final writer repeats authority checks and accepts the prepared artifact and native derived projection only when the current predicate succeeds. A refused, stale, false or errored preparation never acquires current-output authority. It releases only through its existing staging owner. Native acquisition and atomic acceptance for both output owners remain implementation prerequisites.

The proposed v1 retained response in `approval_request.response` is SQL NULL until a complete valid admitted response is applied. The new versioned CLI path stores a closed JSON envelope with integer `version = 1`, `origin = submitted` or `expiry_default`, and `canonical_base64`. The last member is canonical padded standard Base64 of the exact canonical UTF-8 StructuredOutput bytes of the admitted typed response snapshot, at most 1,048,576 decoded bytes and 1,398,104 characters. The eventual retained envelope must be admitted natively before reading or decoding it. Freeze the snapshot after existing ingress normalization; the envelope preserves that snapshot across SQLite and PostgreSQL, including JSON null. No legacy numeric kind or original ingress token is reconstructed. Native readers check size, version, member set and exact Base64 re-encoding before releasing the admitted response. Read adapters expose the decoded logical response through the existing selector and refuse corrupt, unsupported or unavailable content. Their public activation remains with the approval surface. Keep complete valid admitted responses even when the predicate returns false or errors. The response envelope supplies audit retention, not an accepted artifact marker or authority. On success the existing result owner saves the response output with its independently retained canonical payload, contract and frozen CEL projection.

The automatic path keeps `approval_request.status = expired` and `approver` NULL even when it succeeds. It uses the existing `approval.expired` event, with exact `approval_id`, `wait_id`, `on_expiry`, and actual `node_status` in its payload, under the scheduler system actor. A completed CEL error adds `evaluation_error` with the safe record. It does not write `approval.responded` or count as human approval. This is the proposed [ADR-0006 audit exception](../../docs/decisions/0006-graph-definition-dsl-and-yaml-representation.md#proposed-automatic-cli-approval-expiry-audit). An authorized human response keeps the ordinary immutable approver audit and `approval.responded` semantics, including `failed` and the saved response on an applied CEL error. Scheduler restart or repeat expiry of a closed pair returns without a second event, new output, hold release, or changed deadline.

## 4. Address ownership and closure

Only the exact pending current CLI request's open delivery retains its form and complete bound context for delivery. The form is the immutable inline saved value and needs no separate store address. Name the root, both command-capture owners, and content-bearing summary by their exact accepted owner identities. Include the existing projection owner's exact identity in the bound context and protect its native retained payload while held. Under the run lock and original ordered scopes, acquire the unique complete ordered set of physical addresses of those owners, plus any additional physical addresses written by the same transition, before locking/modifying saved owners. Derive the set from admitted storage identity and full digest, independent of aliases. Missing/unavailable sources, address-generation mismatch, or a pending deletion fence prevent the new hold. Recheck the exact context manifest under the lock. The [shared address eligibility](execution-content.md#24-eligibility-and-indexes) guards every owner writer and delete admission.

Extend the shared hold relation for this purpose. The key is the exact approval request ID, accepted content owner type and ID, and reason `approval_delivery`, with the complete physical address for physical content and an exact native derived owner for the CEL view. This new reason and owner predicate need explicit approval. Keep an address-leading live-hold lookup and a request-leading owner lookup. The same physical address may represent several accepted owners. Release each of this request's holds without removing the protection of another live holder or an independently eligible owner. Inline form delivery eligibility follows the same current request/wait/revision predicate. Releasing that eligibility removes the form from delivery, while retaining its authorized historical snapshot.

Opening acquires these holds with revision 1. Every closing path for the exact pair, including direct response, ordinary expiry, supersession, cancellation and enclosing expiry, atomically sets revision 2, revokes current observation/response authority, and releases the original delivery holds under the same address phase. The closing writer joins the original request's live holds by their retained identities and rereads all touched holds before writes. A normal request owns at most four logical physical content holds (root, stdout, stderr and summary) and one native projection hold, independent of aliases or the size of retained history. It refuses a partial union. Cancellation stays ordered by the enclosing scope's original sticky latch; incomplete closure remains due until finished. Replaying a closed owner changes no revision, audit or holds. If an already prepared user response or default loses authority, dispose of its private preparation once actual work/reads close. The existing [#1444 cancellation writer](scheduler.md#metadata-expiry-and-recovery-implementation-1444) must be composed with this typed close for v1; legacy simple JSON updates cannot perform this release. Release changes hold eligibility and cannot delete bytes, renew retention, revoke other owners, or clear publication or physical execution reservations.

Every attempt to open or close has one finite admitted deadline for its metadata and detached preparation phases, present through actual native disposal. Whole-document validation, reads of artifact payloads and provider calls stay outside metadata resources. Use bounded native read acquisition and dispose before acquiring host or provider resources. No failure, timeout or restart is proof that an outstanding reader, writer, portal or effect released.

## 5. Delivery and verification

The accepted CommandResult writer and both capture/derived owners require the shared custody/cleanup producer, a sealed request and an authenticated original runtime observation. Deliver actual submit/handle/progress authority and replay before acceptance where it is a producer prerequisite, under a reconciled, separately approved #1454 plan. Then deliver authenticated terminal reporting over already accepted artifacts and actual runtime, dispatcher and publication closure. Settlement consumes that report once. A focused test may compose real production transitions to prove report-to-settlement and accepted context ownership without the scheduler loop. The approval plan needs complete accepted producers and address/hold support before activation. The production-line count includes native readers, formats, migrations, holds and shared response/failure ownership. Reconcile the complete count under the 1,000-line limit before any runtime edits.

The #1447 runtime delivery owns the real opening and expiry integration, including the shared internal automatic-response path. The public approval owner independently activates human-response DTOs, authorization and CLI/MCP parity. The full #1448 tick still depends on #1446 and #1447. Its real restart runs a fresh loop over the same database, with recovery first, independent due sources and fresh bounded fixed-point marker acknowledgement. It also owns the actual cancellation and shutdown of the old loop before starting the replacement.
