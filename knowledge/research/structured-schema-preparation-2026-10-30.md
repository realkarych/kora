## Complete preparation delivery under #501

The completion implements `prepare_structured_schema_input(value)`, exported by `kora.ir`, over the exact frozen `StructuredSchemaPreparationInput`. Its fields are `declared_type`, `type_contracts`, `inline_schema`, `execution_semantics`, `checker_binding` and `preparation_parameters`. Resolved `type_contracts` is an exact native list of exact `{name,builtin_revision,parent,behavior,descriptor,source,schema}` records; inline input is null or `{source,schema}`. Value definitions live in `structured_schema_input`; `structured_schema_preparation` owns orchestration and the entry; `structured_schema_locations` owns original positions, strict decoding/closed targets and the potential-call graph. No structural-model coercion bridges this native boundary.

The pure synchronous operation reuses #500's primitives to prepare a selected non-list StructuredOutput revision-1 ancestry. It detaches original schema/source/descriptor/behavior data, derives ordered constraint/checker roles, closes references over original admitted positions and checks the potential-call DAG without expanding shared targets. Complete normalized input includes generated wrappers/roles/reference paths and original metadata. Pre-allocation input/work/auxiliary reservations cover the complete call without per-document resets. Exact ordering, primitive charging and phase/refusal precedence remain mandatory. Unsupported execution schemas remain structurally representable under ordinary IR contracts. The accepted normalized representation, ten capacities, algorithms, refusal order, authority and resource rules are unchanged; this delivery accepts no new policy semantics.

The caller retains prior declaration/schema validity, saved-record authenticity, installed-behavior authorization and concurrent-write exclusion. Success yields only opaque immutable `PreparedStructuredSchemaInput` with `accounting: StructuredSchemaPreparationAccounting`. Accounting has nine integer fields: `input_bytes`, `input_nodes`, `input_depth`, `documents`, `positions`, `references`, `work`, `auxiliary_bytes`, `auxiliary_entries`. `StructuredSchemaPreparationRefusal` contains only `reason`, one of `unsupported_binding`, `unsupported_schema` or `preparation_exhausted`. These four value types and the operation are exported from `kora.ir`. Neither child evaluates or accepts a payload, runs behavior hooks, instantiates an evaluator/engine view, writes a saved policy/codec, resolves filesystem/network authority or activates a real consumer. Frozen artifact schema/descriptor/vector bytes, payload admission, dependencies, import permissions and provider/public-surface contracts are unchanged.

The existing caller-bound `ir.validation` logger emits DEBUG `ir.structured_schema_preparation.completed`, with four boolean flags `prepared`, `unsupported_binding`, `unsupported_schema`, `preparation_exhausted` and the nine integer accounting slots. A successful result sets only `prepared` true and supplies actual accounting. Refusal sets its sole reason flag true, `prepared` false and the nine slots to fixed zero placeholders. Those zeros expose no refusal counters and make no assertion that refused work/allocation was zero; the public refusal object still contains only its reason. The event carries no original content or exception chain. Internal errors retain the existing caller-owned failure boundary.

The ten preparation capacities are approved only for this operation. The earlier [thirteen-value envelope](structured-output-effective-schema-design-2026-10-30.md) remains historical research for the future saved policy. `max_numeric_intermediate_bits`, `max_evaluation_work` and `max_evaluation_depth` remain unapproved. Complete evaluation accounting, all numerical parameters, handler/adapter charging and safe engine execution must be accepted before evaluation implementation or executable publication. `kora.structured-output-schema-policy.v1` remains reserved/unavailable, and preparation supplies no missing defaults.
