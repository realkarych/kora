## Internal primitive implementation under #500

The first child implements only two internal modules. `structured_schema_work` owns `PreparationMeter`, content-free `PreparationRefused`, frozen `PARAMETERS`, `merge_sort` and `binary_lookup`. `structured_schema_input` owns the `SchemaArena` builder, frozen `SchemaNode` and `FrozenSchemaArena`: `snapshot(value, depth=1)` admits a native occurrence and returns its node ID; `freeze(root)` detaches an immutable arena; builder `encode(root)` and `FrozenSchemaArena.encode(meter)` produce charged canonical bytes.
