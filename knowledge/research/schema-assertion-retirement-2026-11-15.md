# Retirement of unused schema assertions and equality

Issue [#1625](https://github.com/realkarych/kora/issues/1625) retires the next private evaluator group after the parameter wrappers in #1619. The working source baseline is `80dd8bca4ebff5c37f7a985e43b20f41d41d73e1`; the PR records the actual merged dependency/base before publication. Delivery follows the accepted [standard-schema contract](../specs/schema-validation.md) and [ADR-0027](../../docs/decisions/0027-bounded-effective-schema-validation.md), without an early-merge or consumer-activation exception.

## Caller and resource ownership

The removed production modules are `structured_schema_assertions.py` (333 lines), `structured_schema_equality.py` (211) and `structured_schema_collection_predicates.py` (189), all in `src/kora/ir`: 733 deletions, no additions. Source/test/configuration and exported-name inventory found only the group's internal equality imports and its five dedicated test modules. The cursor test's sole external `frozen_literal` helper use is replaced by direct real SchemaArena snapshot/freeze setup. Historical research and lane-duration rows remain; the lane planner schedules collected tests rather than deleted historical modules.

The original preparation, policy, source/reference readers and immutable history remain. `structured_schema_cursors`, `structured_schema_numbers` and `structured_schema_evaluation_work` and their tests remain for their separate later retirement. This change adds no accepting output/parameter consumer, provider binding, saved format, storage/custody operation, Worker report or scheduler settlement.

## Preserved contracts and changed implementation status

The new OUTPUT-domain matrices use the real shared validator with its exact installed output binding. They preserve scalar kind, mathematical comparisons, decimal/subnormal divisibility, recursive const/enum/uniqueness, Unicode code-point length and immediate container cardinality. Existing PARAMETERS-specific cases from #1619 remain independent; shared both-domain membership/decimal tests retain their explicit domain binding. OUTPUT-only cases are not parameter-domain evidence.

Historical v1 work, frame, handle and traversal schedules remain specified but unavailable. Their exact thresholds, branch-sensitive exhaustion and post-caller-GC observations are not transferred to v2. V2 admits the whole source/value and the full possible certificate before Boolean evaluation. In particular, malformed unvisited arguments no longer become valid because a primitive was inapplicable, and a first match cannot waive potential work. The 16385-member object from a former private count test exceeds the accepted v2 member limit. No existing gate is silently reclassified.

The two structural count matrices remain under their real `validate_type_schemas` owner with identical decorators and assertions: 24 string-length cases and 24 container-count cases. Existing cursor/model/arena tests retain NaN, both infinities, surrogate text/key, hostile subclasses, over-width integers and cycles; raw NaN/duplicate/surrogate cases retain SO002. The existing model depth test retains inclusive16/refusing17 and explicitly asserts SO003. Native model admission and historical wider source support are independent of new executable policy limits.

New semantic/source cases are in `tests/ir/test_standard_schema_retained_cases.py`; existing standard source/admission/lifetime/semantics suites remain. `test_standard_schema_numeric_view_preserves_decimal_semantics` also checks both domains and original serialization. `test_standard_schema_capacity_boundaries`, `test_standard_schema_combined_admission_refuses_before_construction`, `test_standard_schema_numeric_intermediates_are_reserved_before_fraction` and `test_standard_schema_unique_capacity_applies_only_when_reachable` own the replacement limits. `test_standard_schema_errors_release_owned_values`, `test_standard_schema_refusal_drops_original_exception_graph`, content-exclusion and sink-failure tests own shared mechanism evidence. Actual output/parameter composition, saved readers and deployment completion still require their own checks.
