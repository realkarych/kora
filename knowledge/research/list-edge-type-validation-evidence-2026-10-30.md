## Delivered boundary

`validate_edge_types` retains the structurally validated, unchanged `GraphDocument` precondition. It visits each input in the root and every stored body once, including unused bodies, without expanding calls. An authored path, even empty, or a reserved source excludes the input before lookup. Missing ordinary producers or outputs are unresolved. Each resolved endpoint is parsed iteratively into its base name and list depth; malformed spelling or unavailable saved base ancestry leaves the comparison unresolved, even when depths differ. Known endpoints are compatible exactly when their depths match and the producer base is identical to, or a saved descendant of, the consumer base. Optional inputs do not waive compatibility.

`TypeShape` is an internal frozen value. The extracted parser preserves `base_type` and declaration diagnostics; it relies on the existing structural spelling precondition rather than defining another raw-input boundary. It does not unwrap lists in `StructuredAncestry` or output-role checks. Parsing uses offsets and counters, without recursion, a depth stack or a new depth cap. Each edge scans both endpoint spellings, including repeated scans of a shared producer spelling. The ancestry forest, global preorder intervals and lookup behavior remain unchanged.

Checked, unresolved and excluded edges partition all inputs. `edge_types_valid` means no established nominal mismatch; unresolved prerequisites may coexist with that scoped success. Fixed `IR003` messages identify the consumer input's type and retain only its safe source, with null semantic offset. Invalid prerequisites retain declaration/link ownership, while independent known mismatches continue. Exported authored data remains unchanged.

No schema, provider, payload or current registry alias is consulted. This extension does not establish inherited schema compatibility, path/reserved-source typing, body interfaces, exports, collect/fan-out typing, payload admission or execution readiness. AgentSession, StructuredOutput and CommandResult aggregate-consumer prerequisites remain active; no aggregate budget, list materialization or API/CLI/MCP operation is added.

## Diagnostic observations and limits

`test_edge_type_resource_observations` retains all eight existing workloads and adds 1,000 depth-one list edges, 100 depth-2,000 list edges and 3,000 depth-two list mismatches. Each of the eleven workloads receives one warm-up and three untraced timings: 33 samples. Six mixed post-GC batches record current traced heap and cumulative peak since tracing began. The existing Report/Sample JUnit property is `edge_type_resources`; input construction and traced timings are excluded, the ancestry index is per-call, and RSS is unavailable on macOS. Local measurements run separately from other local checks; hosted environment limitations remain visible.

Exact traversal/category counts, bounded output and actual resource release are blocking. Time, process-wide heap and RSS are diagnostic without numerical thresholds, convergence claims or a general leak-freedom guarantee. Work scales with the saved ancestry index plus visited inputs and the total endpoint spelling scanned per edge, including repeated producer spellings; counters do not measure characters or every machine operation. Temporary shape storage consists of offsets/counters and the current extracted bases, while existing index and collector storage keep their previous ownership. No absolute latency/memory quota or interruption guarantee is introduced.

A demonstrated contract defect, retained owned resource or material unexplained growth triggers bounded investigation. Diagnostic outliers alone do not add campaigns or merge gates. Independent correctness, security and performance review covers the actual diff and evidence; one complete successful final-head CI supplies supported-platform and database coverage without activating deferred aggregate consumers.
