## Result and ownership boundaries

Descriptors and directories enter an exit stack immediately after acquisition; helper release and task cancellation/join ownership precede channel or FIFO waits. Local observers still dispose when signaling or settlement refuses. Cleanup failures and unexpected completed-task faults remain visible. Deliberately adverse storage tasks have strong ownership until parent-owned child teardown, so collection cannot turn an intended live-task refusal into success.

Live/pair probes transfer a private typed record bounded to two process registrations and 64 KiB. The caller validates complete identities and cardinality, then registers every observer and independent cleanup callback before sending ACK. The probe retains its real owner until ACK. Rejected/partial handoffs or failed ACKs grant no signaling authority: stdin closure and probe-owned cleanup happen first. Journal bytes, including corrupt bytes retained as evidence, never provide kill authority.

Recovered-domain fixtures own listeners before bind, children/anchors before accept and accepted controls before receive. They close the complete control set before waiting for retained children. Early identity failure requires the actual reaper's fixed private absence witness; a reaped leader with a live member supplies no false empty-group witness. One lazy actual-clock five-second cleanup deadline covers native/sibling finalizers, and the caller's actual thirty-second report deadline remains. Shielded complete teardown survives repeated cancellation and preserves original errors. Expired-barrier and cancellation cases assert native process/group absence and direct-child reaping before fallback can repair a leak. Completed-task coverage checks helper closure, direct-child status, exception context and exact descriptors without an added native group scan. Signal-refusal coverage proves local-handle disposal while deliberately retaining unsettled process state; its independent emergency finalizer later stops and reaps the direct child. Refusal supplies no successful-settlement claim.

The live/pair fixture's injected decision clock avoids coupling short cooperative decisions to host scheduling of real helpers. Advancing that clock proves no physical closure: actual native readiness, CLOSED, reaping, empty groups and zero observation handles are still required. Production timing and authority are unchanged. The [lifetime ABI](../specs/conformance-lifetime.md), [ADR-0005](../../docs/decisions/0005-eleven-protocols-with-conformance-suites.md) and [current verification guidance](../../docs/development.md) remain the owners; this test-fixture repair supplies no hostile-process isolation guarantee.
