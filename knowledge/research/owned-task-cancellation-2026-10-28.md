## Result and current owners

The old timer-join helper could swallow cancellation of its caller. The repair preserves caller cancellation and target faults while joining the target; a target fault remains primary with simultaneous caller cancellation retained as context. The client writer also joins its sibling timer. Endpoint teardown settles readers, writers and every handler before releasing input charges, wire slots, transport bytes and resource entries. Temporary aggregates remain bounded by admitted work; there is no new background owner or execution authority.

An UNKNOWN report can precede completion of a cleanup effect. Test-owner disposal therefore joins finite recovery dependencies inside its existing five-second budget before releasing local resources; it neither cancels those dependencies nor converts UNKNOWN into CLOSED. The original error survives disposal. Feedback-expiry diagnostics retain at most six monotonic phase offsets and sixteen top frames, no child content, credentials, paths, arguments, environment, exception text or locals, and add no wait or output sink. The twenty-second observer remains unchanged.
