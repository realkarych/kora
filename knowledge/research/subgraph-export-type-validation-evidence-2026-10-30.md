## Delivered boundary

`validate_export_types` accepts a structurally validated `GraphDocument` whose caller-owned maps remain unchanged during the synchronous call. It visits every stored definition once, including unused definitions and absent/empty output maps, without expanding call instances. Each export resolves its producer and named output only in its own body. Missing links are unresolved. `TypeAncestry.accepts_type` parses both endpoint spellings and requires both saved bases to have valid ancestry before comparing list depth and nominal ancestry. Malformed spelling or unavailable ancestry is unresolved, even for identical unknown names or unequal depths. Known types match exactly when depths agree and the producer base is identical to or a saved descendant of the export base.

The shared helper preserves direct-edge precedence, counters, messages and event fields. Base-only ancestry, index construction, declaration parsing and non-list output roles retain their behavior. No schema, behavior implementation, registry alias or payload is consulted. Neither exported data nor the caller's snapshot is changed.

`ExportTypeResult` retains counts and bounded copied diagnostics. Compared and unresolved exports partition visited exports; `export_types_valid` means no established mismatch, not successful prerequisites or overall graph validity. Fixed IR003 identifies the body's export type path, with only revalidated body source and null node/offset. Unsafe body source has no graph or producer fallback. Existing declaration/link owners keep prerequisite diagnostics; independent known mismatches continue.

Ordinary wrapper input/output mapping, output preservation/completeness, fan-out over/collect, path/reserved-source typing, inherited effective-schema evaluation, aggregate materialization and public admission are outside this pass. ADR-0027 and AgentSession/StructuredOutput/CommandResult aggregate-consumer gates remain active. No dependency, persisted representation, package layer, public API/CLI/MCP operation or execution authority changes.

## Diagnostic observations and limits

`test_export_type_resource_observations` records the existing Report/Sample format as JUnit suite property `export_type_resources`. Ten workloads cover 1/100/1,000 scalar exports, 1,000 depth-two list exports, 1,000 exports sharing depth-5,000 ancestry and distinct ancestors, 1,000 unresolved exports with depth-1,000 ancestry, 1,000 calls sharing one export, 1,000 unused definitions, 100 depth-2,000 list exports and 3,000 list mismatches. One warm-up and three untraced timings per workload produce 30 samples. Six mixed post-GC batches record settled traced heap and cumulative peak since tracing began; RSS is recorded when available and is unavailable on macOS. Construction and traced timing are excluded. Local observations run separately from other local checks; hosted contention and recorder/runtime allocations remain limitations.

Exact counters, bounded diagnostics and owned-resource release are blocking. Time, process-wide heap/RSS and cumulative peaks are diagnostic without thresholds, percentiles, convergence claims, general leak-freedom or operational-envelope guarantees. Work comprises one saved index plus visited definitions/exports and all endpoint spellings scanned per export, including repeated producer spellings. Counters do not count characters or every machine operation. Temporary storage includes index entries, current shapes/base strings and the bounded collector; no unique-name-only work claim is made.

A demonstrated contract defect, retained owned resource or material unexplained growth triggers bounded investigation. Diagnostic outliers alone add no campaigns or merge gates. Independent correctness, security and performance review covers the actual diff and evidence; one successful complete final-head CI supplies supported-platform and database coverage without activating deferred consumers.
