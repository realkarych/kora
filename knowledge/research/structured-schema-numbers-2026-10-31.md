## Ownership and reporting limits

Numerical helpers retain no caller containers, global caches, engine instances, descriptors, tasks, threads or processes. Fixed refusal/result representations exclude operands, coefficients and authored paths. An unexpected exception may retain local frames only through its caller-owned traceback until disposal; reporting must not format those locals or native chains. No per-number diagnostic is added. The future evaluator/composition owns its one completion event, while real consumers retain their own diagnostic and lifecycle activation obligations.

The numerical trust boundary is untrusted bounded scalar input and future schema-controlled work under TM-04/TM-10; fixed content-free outcomes cover TM-06/TM-07 and immutable interpretation covers TM-12.
