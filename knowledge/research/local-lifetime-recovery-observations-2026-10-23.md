# Local lifetime recovery observations

Checked 2026-10-23. This record describes implementation evidence for issue #247; it does not broaden the local cleanup contract.

## macOS process-group enumeration

Apple XNU source was inspected at commit `f6217f891ac0bb64f3d375211650a4c1ff8ca1ea`. The [libproc wrapper](https://github.com/apple-oss-distributions/xnu/blob/f6217f891ac0bb64f3d375211650a4c1ff8ca1ea/libsyscall/wrappers/libproc/libproc.c#L40-L54) maps a native error to a zero return. Zero alone therefore cannot distinguish an empty group from failure. The [kernel implementation](https://github.com/apple-oss-distributions/xnu/blob/f6217f891ac0bb64f3d375211650a4c1ff8ca1ea/bsd/kern/proc_info.c#L337-L457) enumerates the selected list under the process-list lock and returns the number of copied bytes, including zero for an empty result.

The native reader clears captured errno immediately before the valid nonnull-buffer call and captures it immediately afterward. Only zero bytes with zero captured error is accepted as an empty enumeration. Negative, malformed, full-buffer, native-error and expired observations remain UNKNOWN. Native calls remain nonpreemptible; cooperative checks do not establish a hard syscall duration bound.

The real macOS test `test_already_reaped_empty_group_recovers_with_stale_errno` launches a separately parented process group, observes its actual parent reap it, seeds a previous EPERM value, and requires an affirmative empty enumeration and recovery settlement. This test passed locally. Linux and final CI results remain separately required; source interpretation is not platform execution evidence.

## Recovered authority and disposal

A recovered observer never owns waitpid or old capture descriptors. Each signal requires a fresh exact live original leader and a complete matching group observation. Once that leader exits or is reaped, surviving descendants remain UNKNOWN; no numeric-only group or member signal is attempted. An already-absent leader needs matching host, boot and namespace, two complete empty-group scans and no pending retained watcher. These process facts do not settle earlier journal or filesystem effects.

Successful release and uncertain observer disposal are separate operations. Disposal closes local watches and permanently disables that observer's authority while retaining the external UNKNOWN outcome. The original parent's descriptor scope and the exact invocation claim must be reconciled independently before whole-invocation closure.

## Terminal RPC close

A child inside the scope closed by `close(None)` cannot await a receipt proving its own death. The typed client receives transport-closed failure; the parent retains the close task and obtains the true final ClosureReport. Subtree close returns its actual report when the caller remains outside that subtree. EOF and lost replies initiate or join parent-owned closure and never certify success themselves. The separate-process terminal and EOF tests assert child exit and the parent's final report. Future command composition must coordinate private cooperative cleanup before termination instead of expecting a successful response after its own scope closes.

## Fixed-helper phase ownership

Checked 2026-10-24. Each retained helper now carries its actual birth and exact ordinary operation task or fixed cleanup permit. Finishing, timing out or cancelling the originating waiter does not remove the helper's scope or escalation constraints. Scoped close selects helpers through the recorded operation/resource relation or cleanup permit; only invocation close includes global bootstrap/publication origins. An unrelated sibling close grants no signalling authority.

A cleanup helper receives its own finite allowance within every owning control's original phases. For birth B, that control's cooperative C, TERM T and final F, let R=F-B. Its TERM instant is S=min(F-2,max(C,B+min(configured cooperative nanoseconds,R//4))); KILL is K=min(F-1,max(S+1,T,S+min(configured TERM nanoseconds,(F-S)//3))). The retained origin stores monotonically earliest S, K and F across eligible enclosing controls. Completing an earlier control cannot widen those constraints. A queued cleanup effect carries the same narrowed final and rechecks it before helper launch. With fewer than four nanoseconds remaining, no fresh cleanup helper starts. A positive accepted float can truncate to zero nanoseconds, making TERM immediately due at birth; no public minimum is invented. Once final is elapsed, missed phases do not authorize fresh identity work or signals.

The existing driver wakes on helper installation and uses these instants in its existing earliest-deadline wait. There is no per-helper task, timer or watcher. Exact identity verification remains required for every signal. Cleanup origins hold their permit rather than redundantly retaining the completed cleanup task and its possible publication traceback. Ordinary and bootstrap origins refer to already-owned canonical tasks. Metadata admission prepays 2 KiB for each possible retained helper slot: max_outstanding_operations plus the single cleanup effect. Both origins and distinct permits have explicit inventory counts. This is logical accounting; native/Python peak and retained-memory measurements must still validate the composition.

The real FIFO regression initially missed TERM for selected acquisition, removal and publication helpers, while the unrelated-sibling case passed. Corrected phase cases observe TERM acknowledgement, continued liveness, subsequent KILL, actual exit/reap and the independent claim remaining busy until its owner releases it. Exact arithmetic cases cover earlier enclosing deadlines, retained minima, small/zero intervals and largest finite float conversion without a floating multiplication overflow.

Enabling actual cleanup-helper escalation exposed a distinct success-test timing assumption. With the existing 0.1-second cooperative allowance, an installed normal cleanup helper received TERM exactly 100,000,000 ns after birth and exited with status -15; the parent correctly returned UNKNOWN after about104 ms. Normal success compositions now use the existing injected decision clock with independent real completion/exit/group-observation guards, keeping all configured durations and CLOSED assertions. Real phase and adversarial-budget tests retain their actual signal/deadline obligations. A diagnostic's first attempt used macOS's lexical /var temporary alias and failed anchored bootstrap; resolving that test path corrected setup, independently of the phase observation.

Test failure cleanup has a separate obligation from a product UNKNOWN report. A real prepared/activated-process regression demonstrated that expiring the decision clock and settling only helpers left the process owner unreleased. The shared test guardian now settles exact process groups, drains direct captures and reaps/releases direct children under an independent real completion bound before releasing its directories and claim. The original test error and product UNKNOWN remain unchanged. Affirmative exited helper anchors are also excluded from escalation diagnostics: an already-exited no-op must not be reported as a live-resource signal.

## Finite large clock targets

Checked 2026-10-24. Adding individually accepted finite phase durations can produce an integer nanosecond target whose conversion to float seconds overflows. The real SystemClock regression reproduced that OverflowError. SystemClock now bounds each individual sleep interval in integer nanoseconds to (2**31 - 1) seconds before float conversion, then rechecks the original absolute target after waking. This is an internal timer interval, not a public duration limit. The same coroutine owns at most one asyncio timer; a further interval is needed only after about 68 years, rather than through frequent polling.

Actual tests retain one timer and its future for a huge target, cancel the wait and verify both are cancelled without an active coroutine frame. Cancellation of an asyncio handle does not imply immediate removal from its lazy internal heap. An expired target completes without suspension, and an ordinary positive target returns only after the original instant. Client acquisition and close construction convert each finite phase independently to integer nanoseconds before addition. Real socket cases keep huge ordinary/close waits pending through caller cancellation, then release client tasks, records and bytes on disconnect. Gate header representation remains a separate boundary; clock correctness alone does not establish large-duration process launch.
