# Design review record, 2026-10-15

> File names in this record are those of the design documents at the time of the review. The `architecture.md` section references below resolve to the [pre-consolidation detailed architecture](https://github.com/realkarych/kora/blob/1baf9891af1d8dc69e4e33dd7bc2af22237072c5/knowledge/architecture/detailed.md), which preserves their numbering. The other files have since moved: `design-decisions.md` is [design-decisions-2026-10-15.md](design-decisions-2026-10-15.md), and the other files are in [../specs/](../specs/). The decisions are grouped and explained in [docs/decisions/](../../docs/decisions/README.md).

Three independent reviews of the design documents (now `knowledge/specs/` and `knowledge/architecture/`) were run with different lenses: **simplicity** (fewer concepts, one way to do each thing), **production readiness** (failure modes, security, operations) and **industry practice** (alignment with how comparable systems are built). The owner decided the contested findings; the resulting decisions are DD-27 to DD-31 in [design-decisions.md](design-decisions-2026-10-15.md). This file records every finding with its disposition. "Applied" findings are reflected in the design documents, the IR schema and the examples.

## Cycle 2: consistency, implementability, threat model, simplicity pass 2

The second cycle ran four reviews over the revised documents. Contested points were decided by the owner (DD-32 to DD-37); threat-model controls, simplicity items, consistency fixes and implementability defaults were applied as proposed. Dispositions below; "applied" means reflected in the documents, the schema and the examples.

|---|---|---|---|

|---|---|---|
